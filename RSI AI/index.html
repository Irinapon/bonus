Problem & Solution01

Built to avoid the usual AI translation pitfalls

Most AI interpretation tools force a trade-off: painfully slow output, or fast output that doesn't actually make sense. RSI X AI is built for both — ultra-low latency and coherent, accurate translation, in a lightweight tool that doesn't get in your way.

  1. Waiting up to 30 seconds for a translation.By then the speaker has moved on. RSI X AI keeps latency at 3 seconds or less, so listeners stay in the conversation.
  2. Fast output that's garbled — or worse.Speed means nothing if the meaning breaks. RSI X AI is tuned for coherent, accurate translation, not word-by-word guessing.
  3. Heavy tools that get in the way.No bots in your meeting, no bloated installs. One light tool that runs on a weak connection and barely touches the battery.

FAQ07

Straight answers before you get in touch

What's the difference between this and RSI Exchange / human interpretation?

RSI Exchange connects your event with professional human interpreters — the right choice when nuance, tone and high-stakes judgment matter. RSI X AI is an AI tool from the same team: it supports interpreters as a real-time aid, or handles less-sensitive meetings and conferences on its own.

How many languages are supported?

70+ languages, for both voice interpretation and live captions.

What platforms does it work with?

Any of them. RSI X AI listens to audio and video from any online platform — Zoom, Teams, Google Meet or anything else — with no plugin needed. Listeners get the output through the RSI X AI app or a web page.

How is my data handled?

Audio processed by RSI X AI is never used to train AI models and is not retained long-term.

Is this really as fast as it claims?

Yes. In our own timing tests, RSI X AI runs at a maximum latency of 3 seconds — comparable to human interpretation. The underlying model processes speech in 100-millisecond chunks to stay as close to real time as possible.
