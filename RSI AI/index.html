Problem & Solution01

Built to avoid the usual AI translation pitfalls

AI interpretation often makes people choose between speed and meaning. If the translation arrives too late, the conversation has already moved on. If it arrives too quickly but loses the meaning, it creates a different problem.

RSI X AI is built to keep both in view: fast enough to follow, coherent enough to understand, and light enough to stay out of the way.

  1. Too slowWhen translation takes 30 seconds, the speaker is already somewhere else.
  2. Too unclearFast output is not useful if names, numbers and meaning get lost.
  3. Too heavyNo unnecessary bots, plugins or complicated setup on the meeting platform.

FAQ07

Straight answers before you get in touch

What's the difference between RSI X AI and RSI Exchange?

RSI X AI provides fast AI interpretation and captions for situations where a professional interpreter is not available or where a full interpretation setup would be unnecessary. RSI Exchange provides professional human interpreters for high-stakes events where nuance, tone and judgment are critical.

How many languages are supported?

RSI X AI supports more than 70 languages for live interpretation and captions. Language availability may vary depending on the selected mode and event setup.

What platforms does it work with?

RSI X AI can listen to audio and video from any online meeting or event platform. No plugin or bot is required on the original platform. The interpreted output is delivered through the RSI X AI app or web page.

How is my data handled?

Audio processed by RSI X AI is not used to train AI models on our paid-tier setup. Data retention follows the applicable service settings. Stronger zero-retention wording will only be used once specifically confirmed.

Is it really as fast as it claims?

Internal end-to-end timing tests measure a maximum latency of 3 seconds, comparable to human interpretation. Speech is processed in 100-millisecond chunks to keep translation close to real time.

Can RSI X AI be used alongside professional interpreters?

Yes. RSI X AI can serve as a real-time aid, helping surface terminology, numbers and names while a professional interpreter remains in control of the event.
